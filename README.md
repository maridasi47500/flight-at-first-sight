# flight-at-first-sight
Hey
Flight at First Sight
Meet people, share music, and discover new sounds—from the airport gate to the train seat.
Concept
Flight at First Sight is a social–musical app designed for travelers who want more than just a journey:
Meet at the plane/train/airport:  
Connect with nearby travelers before boarding or during the trip.
Be in the “competition”:  
Join friendly music challenges, playlists battles, or style contests with other users.
Family roots in music:  
Highlight your musical background—like having family members who play instruments.
Core features
Choose a music style  
Define your favorite genres (classical, jazz, rap, rock, etc.) to match with like‑minded travelers.
Create a music profile
Bio: Who you are as a listener or musician
Instruments: What you play (e.g., violin, guitar, piano)
Influences: Artists, composers, or bands you love
Learn music
Curated learning paths (beginner to advanced)
Tips, exercises, and recommended resources based on your style
Discover violin makers (luthiers)
Realistic, location‑based suggestions of violin makers
Notes on which makers are best suited for certain styles (baroque, jazz, folk, classical)
Mobile first  
Designed for smartphones so you can use it on the go—at the gate, on the train, or mid‑flight (offline modes where possible).
Use cases
You’re at the airport:  
Open the app, see who’s around, match by music taste, and start a chat or playlist exchange.
You want to improve your playing:  
Get style‑specific tips and find a violin maker who can set up your instrument for that sound.
You’re from a musical family:  
Showcase your story and connect with others who grew up with music at home.
Roadmap
v0.1: Basic profiles, style selection, nearby traveler discovery
v0.2: Music learning modules and style‑based recommendations
v0.3: Luthier/violin‑maker directory with realistic filters
v1.0: Full release with competitions, badges, and offline travel mode
